# Ongoing Management

## View Scan History and Past Findings

**Flow Type:** ongoing_management
**Scope Status:** supporting_for_first_version
**Trigger:** Developer wants to check previous scan results or revisit a finding they triaged earlier.
**Outcome:** Developer accesses the investigation history for a scan run and can filter or search past findings.

### Steps

1. **[system]** Update the finding list in real time
   - Apply the selected filters or search term and refresh the displayed findings immediately.
